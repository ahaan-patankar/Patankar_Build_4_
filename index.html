How To Make A PBJ

  • Ingredients
  • Directions

Ingredients

2 slices of bread 2 tablespoons peanut butter 2 tablespoons jelly or jam (grape or strawberry)

Directions

1. Place two slices of bread on a plate. 2. Spread peanut butter evenly on one slice. 3. Spread jelly on the other slice. 4. Put the two slices together with the peanut butter and jelly facing each other. 5. Cut the sandwich in half if desired. 6. Enjoy your PB&J sandwich!p
